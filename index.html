OrgKit

Salesforce developer toolkit for Chrome — Session Workbench, dual-org Org Compare, SOQL, schema, Apex, and Setup utilities. Uses your open Salesforce tab session in the browser only.

What you get

  • Session Workbench — quick launch, continue, pinned SOQL, scratch pad
  • Org Compare — objects, profiles, permission sets, flows, Apex, and more
  • Day-to-day tools — SOQL/Tooling, Describe, metadata open, package.xml, Apex helpers
